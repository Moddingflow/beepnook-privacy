WHAT STAYS ON YOUR PHONE

The game stores your progress (day, coins, orders, tools, decor), a backup copy of the save and your settings (language, volumes, haptics, text size, hints) on the device. These files never leave the phone and are not sent to us. If app backup is on in your phone, the system may copy them to your Google or Apple cloud under your own settings.

ADS (GOOGLE ADMOB)

Between days and on buttons with a video sign, the game shows ads through Google AdMob. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (EEA and UK), and Google LLC elsewhere. To serve ads, Google processes:
• the advertising ID (Android Advertising ID, or IDFA on iPhone) and the app set ID;
• the IP address and the rough location it gives;
• device model, system version and language;
• which ads were shown and tapped;
• diagnostics of the ad module.
Purposes: showing and counting ads, preventing fraud and, if you agree, personalised ads. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25 TDDDG); without it Google shows non-personalised or limited ads on the basis of our legitimate interest in a free game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). More: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

CONSENT AND HOW TO CHANGE IT

No ad is requested before the consent check, which runs at the end of the first day in the game. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google shows a consent form; you can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the "Ad privacy options" button at the bottom of this screen. In US states with privacy laws, Google shows a message where you can opt out of sharing data for ads. On iPhone, the system asks whether to allow tracking; without it the IDFA is not shared. On Android you can reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone's settings: Google → Ads.

PURCHASES

Purchases (Remove Ads, coin packs) are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms. We never see your card, name or address. The game gets only the order details from the store: which product, the transaction number and its status, so it can deliver the purchase and restore it after a reinstall. Legal basis: performing the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
Purchases run through the Unity In-App Purchasing library by Unity Technologies. It may send Unity technical data about how purchases work (session events, device and system details, project and session identifiers, the rough location from the IP address, crash and performance data, and the purchase history; an email address only briefly, if the store passes one on) to run and diagnose the service. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in purchases that work (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). More: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

TRANSFERS OUTSIDE THE EU

Google, Apple and Unity may process data in the USA and other countries. Such transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

HOW LONG DATA IS KEPT

The files on your phone stay until you delete the game. We keep nothing on servers: we have none. Google, Apple and Unity keep data under their own privacy policies.

IF YOU WRITE TO US

We use your address and your message only to answer you, and delete the correspondence once the matter is settled, unless the law requires us to keep it longer. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

CHILDREN

The game is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. In the EEA, a parent gives consent to personalised ads for children under 16. If a child has shared data anyway, write to us and we will help remove it.

YOUR RIGHTS

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict its processing, to data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing already done. As we keep no data ourselves, most requests are carried out by Google, Apple and Unity: write to us and we will tell you how. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in the country where you live, or in Saxony (Germany), where the controller is based.

PERMISSIONS

The game uses internet access and the network state (ads and purchases), the advertising ID (Android), the store's billing service and vibration. It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location. The only system prompt that asks for your consent is the tracking question on iPhone.

DELETING YOUR DATA

Uninstall the game and your save and settings go with it. New game starts your progress over. Reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone's settings. Purchases stay in the store's history under its rules.
